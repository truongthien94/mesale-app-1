## Scope

Harden the native Account experience and its Laravel contracts without changing Home, deploying production migrations, or altering production data.

## Implementation

- Confirmed the two previously observed Home files are restored and clean; Home is not part of this batch.
- Collapsed the Account hub into one high-level row for each of `Thông tin tài khoản`, `Tài chính`, `Thông báo`, and `Cài đặt`; the existing Explore, support/legal, logout, and deletion areas remain available.
- Added focused nested Account menus and a themed safe-area-aware React header with an accessible back action and an Account fallback for deep links without navigation history.
- Added native avatar selection through the system Photo Picker, bounded square resize, authenticated upload, preview refresh, and deletion. Camera and broad media permissions are not requested.
- Added server-authoritative referral-code eligibility for the first 72 hours after registration. Existing accounts are not backfilled into the new eligibility window, and the mobile client does not authorize submission from its own clock.
- Removed `avatar_path` from mass-assignment allowlists. Activity-log failures after a committed avatar upload or deletion no longer convert the successful mutation into an HTTP 500 response.

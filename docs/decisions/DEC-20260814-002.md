# DEC-20260814-002 - Keep milestone cards target-first

- Date: 2026-08-14
- Session: `SES-20260814-002`
- Checkpoint: `CKP-20260814-002`
- Status: accepted

## Decision

- Referral and cashback-order milestone cards use Laravel `target_count` as the visible milestone label.
- Long task titles are omitted from those two timeline card types because they can duplicate or conflict with the authoritative target.
- Custom or unrelated task cards continue to render their server-provided title and description.
- Compact visual controls retain extra touch slop rather than increasing their visible height.

## Rationale

- Production already contains task copy that may not match `target_count`; target-first presentation prevents misleading milestone labels.
- The requested compact density is not compatible with repeating multi-line titles inside every timeline card.
- No reward amount, eligibility rule, progress calculation, or claim behavior should be duplicated in the app.

# DEC-20260815-001 - Fit configured milestones without placeholders

- Date: 2026-08-15
- Session: `SES-20260815-001`
- Checkpoint: `CKP-20260815-001`
- Status: accepted

## Decision

- Use a check-in-style strip with a `47dp` slot and `34dp` circular state marker for referral and cashback-order milestones.
- Remove per-node progress/status/button chrome; make the full node the accessible sync/claim control.
- Render only Laravel-provided milestones; do not add empty, sample, or hardcoded reward cards to make a row look fuller.

## Rationale

- The requested check-in-like density requires about seven concise nodes to fit without repeating verbose content.
- Placeholder reward cards could mislead members about available campaigns or earning amounts.
- Laravel remains authoritative for targets, rewards, progress, status, and eligibility.
